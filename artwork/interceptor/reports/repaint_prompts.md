# Texture repaint prompts

Built-in imagegen was used to edit the orthographic views; the resulting images were projected and baked into the existing UV atlas.

## top

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: orthographic projected ALBEDO texture for an existing game spaceship. Image 1 is the edit target: the EXACT top-down render layout that must remain geometrically and pixel-position identical. Image 2 is ONLY a finish/paint reference; do not copy its orientation or shape. Repaint Image 1 into a crisp clean hard-surface spaceship diffuse color image. Preserve exact silhouette, object placement, proportions, voids between struts, all tip positions, engine positions, cockpit position, background color, framing and canvas edges of Image 1. Nose remains at TOP and engines remain at BOTTOM. Preserve cream armor, vermilion angular wing stripes, graphite structural trusses, dark canopy, blue small lights. Replace dirty marbled blurry AI surface noise with smooth warm ivory painted armor, thin precise dark panel seams, tiny restrained mechanical fasteners, clean flat red markings with sharp boundaries, matte graphite machinery. Very light restrained edge wear only. It must be an unlit albedo pass, no lighting gradients, no highlights, no cast shadows, no bloom, no glow spread, no 3D perspective. Do not move, add, remove or reshape any part. The image will be projected directly back onto the exact mesh, so alignment with Image 1 is paramount. Produce a high-resolution square image, same full canvas as Image 1. Do not include text, numbers, watermarks or annotations.

## bottom

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: exact orthographic projected albedo map for a game spaceship. Image 1 is the EDIT TARGET (bottom orthographic view). Preserve its EXACT silhouette, framing, canvas, part placement, proportions, holes, engine positions and orientation. Image 2 is a FINISH REFERENCE ONLY: already cleaned top paint for the SAME ship. Repaint Image 1 with the same clean hard-surface quality as Image 2. Remove blurry marbling, lumpy painted shading, dirt streaks and melted-looking edges from the color. Use smooth warm ivory armor with fine precise restrained dark panel seams, matte graphite mechanical frames with small fasteners and tidy vents, blue small inset light accents; engines are brushed graphite metal with restrained blue outer trim. Keep all original colors and locations of Image 1, including the ivory undersides and structural voids. No new red stripes on undersides or sides. Keep all blue accents at existing positions. Retain the exact gray background from Image 1. No additional parts, no shape changes, no perspective, no lighting gradients, no highlights, no glow, no shadows, no text. This must be an unlit diffuse paint pass, not a shaded render. Clean crisp thin panel detailing without heavy black outlines. A square output with all target pixels in exactly the same positions is essential because it will be projected onto the original 3D mesh.
