# Repaint prompts

Built-in imagegen, six independent edits. Each first image is the corresponding `renders/{view}_repaint_guide.png`; second image is `references/missle fighter.png`.

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: orthographic albedo projection for a game spaceship. Edit the first image only. Preserve EXACT ship silhouette, all part positions, every opening, the camera angle, image framing, scale and flat background pixel color. Clean the noisy reconstructed paint: smooth warm ivory armor panels with crisp fine mechanical seams and restrained edge wear, clean muted brick-red stripes in existing locations, dark graphite machinery with precise mechanical details, dark blue cockpit glass, tiny cyan light strips and cyan engine bands. Remove marbled wrinkles, blotches, brown smears, fake shadows and excessive grunge. Keep the exact twin missile-cell grids and cell counts in the top view. Do not redesign, add or remove parts, move or straighten the ship, change its proportions, or change framing. Flat even albedo lighting, no cast shadows, no bloom, no labels. Second image is material/color reference only; do not adopt its camera or silhouette. Output one square image same framing as first.
