The layered library covers every pairing. Bake a selected pair once, then reuse the cached textures before a match. Ready-made PNGs are included for all presets. This catalog previews actual Blender renders.

Original source files remain unchanged. The “original” preset uses standardized ivory and oxide-red paint; “historical source paint” shows the archived finish. Preview textures are capped at 2048 px. Exported textures retain source resolution. Individually framed models are not to scale. Carrier and carrier modules are excluded.
